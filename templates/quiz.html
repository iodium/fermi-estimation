Instructions

Welcome to the estimation quiz! This is not a traditional quiz where you input an answer. Instead, you answer the question with a range you believe the answer falls in. Even if you get the answer in the range, a smaller range gets more points rewarded than a larger one. Precision is rewarded. If your range does not include the answer, you will still be given points based on how close to the answer your upper and lower bounds are. Score is out of 100.
